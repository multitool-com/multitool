Uploading an image with the wrong dimensions is the fastest way to get awkward crops, blurry previews or cut-off text. This cheat sheet collects the sizes that matter in 2026 — bookmark it and resize once, post everywhere.

## Instagram

| Placement | Size (px) | Aspect ratio |
|---|---|---|
| Square post | 1080 × 1080 | 1:1 |
| Portrait post (max reach) | 1080 × 1350 | 4:5 |
| Stories / Reels | 1080 × 1920 | 9:16 |
| Profile photo | 320 × 320 | 1:1 |

The 4:5 portrait takes the most screen space in the feed — the best default for reach.

## Facebook

| Placement | Size (px) | Aspect ratio |
|---|---|---|
| Link / shared image | 1200 × 630 | ~1.91:1 |
| Feed photo | 1080 × 1350 or 1200 × 630 | 4:5 or landscape |
| Cover | 851 × 315 | ~2.7:1 |
| Profile | 170 × 170 | 1:1 |

## YouTube

| Placement | Size (px) | Aspect ratio |
|---|---|---|
| Thumbnail | 1280 × 720 | 16:9 |
| Channel banner (safe area) | 1546 × 423 | — |
| Profile | 800 × 800 | 1:1 |

Thumbnails under 1280px wide get rejected; keep faces and text large — they render at postage-stamp size on phones.

## Format and file size rules

1. **JPG for photos, PNG for text/logos** on top of solid backgrounds — text stays razor sharp in PNG. WebP is increasingly accepted and 25–35% smaller.
2. **Stay under ~1 MB per image.** Platforms re-compress everything anyway; huge files just upload slowly and lose quality twice.
3. **Export at exactly 2× the display size** for logos/text overlays (crisp on retina screens).
4. **Keep faces and text in the center 80%** — every platform crops edges differently per placement.

## FAQ-style quick answers

**Do these sizes change often?** A few pixels here and there yearly — the aspect ratios are stable for years. When in doubt, 1080-wide images cover 95% of cases.

**Why does my image look blurry after upload?** Either it was smaller than the placement size (platform upscaled it) or above ~5 MB (aggressive re-compression). Hit the recommended size and stay under 1 MB.

**One image for every network?** Square 1080×1080 survives everywhere; portrait 1080×1350 maximizes Instagram/Facebook but gets letterboxed on X. When reach matters, make 2 versions.
